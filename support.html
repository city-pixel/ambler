Ambler support

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/city-pixel/ambler/issues. Tell us the tour, the stop and what happened. To fix a fact in a story, you can also use Add yours on that stop in the app and choose "correction".

Common questions

Do I need data or an account? No. Every tour is in the app and works offline. There is no account.

A story didn't start. Check that Location is allowed for Ambler in Settings and that a walk or Explore is on. You can always tap a stop and play it.

What's free? The first three stops of every tour. After that, buy one tour or the city pass, each a one-time purchase.

Restore purchases. Settings in Ambler, then Restore Purchases. Purchases are shared with your Family Sharing group.

Our 14-day promise. Not happy with a tour? Within 14 days of buying, request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple handles all App Store refunds) and tell us why in an issue. We back every such request.
