Ambler privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026.

Ambler is built to work without knowing who you are. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Location

Ambler uses your location to start the right story when you arrive and to clear the map where you walk. This happens only on your iPhone. Your location and your walking history are not sent to us or to anyone else.

What you choose to send ("Add yours")

If you use Add yours to send a photo, story, comment or correction, it is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) for Ambler, so we can read it. Nothing is sent unless you press send. Photos are re-saved without their metadata (no GPS, camera or time). If you send from the place itself, an approximate location (about 10 m) is attached; never when walking from home. CloudKit gives us an anonymous ID for the sender, not your name or email. We may use what you send, with or without credit, to improve the stories. You can delete anything you sent at any time in Ambler: Settings, What you sent, Take it down.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Data on your phone

Your progress, settings and anything waiting to be sent are stored on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

Children

Ambler does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: github.com/city-pixel/ambler/issues.
